# Reflections

1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?
The initial idea was to build a simple, abstract “constellation” space that generated unique combinations of sounds based on the user’s interactions. While this concept satisfied the project requirements, it lacked a concrete direction. I thus decided to establish stronger boundaries around the UI and functionality. From there, I shifted toward a more traditional interface inspired by Apple’s GarageBand, while focusing on looping functionality similar to apps such as Loopy HD. The work really began to move forward once I refined the concept and established specific constraints, including the scale of the interface and the instruments that should be included. 

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?
This crit highlighted the importance of setting clear constraints and doing requirements research. I learned that defining the scope early is important when working with an agentic workflow, as it provides makes each iteration more purposeful. Going forward, I'd like to be a developer who is deliberate about defining problems before implementation.
