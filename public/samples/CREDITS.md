# Sample credits

Real-audio recordings backing the Loop Library's `style` and `sample` clips
(app.js §6/7). Each recording is decoded once and offered as two clips: a
`style` clip, a longer excerpt automatically trimmed to a beat boundary and
resampled live to the BPM slider via `playbackRate`; and a `sample` clip
(labeled "(Live)" in the shelf), the untouched full recording looping at its
own native speed, offered as an alternative to the resampled excerpt.
